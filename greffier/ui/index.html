Greffier

the clerk's record of a Scala 3 compile: every phase, every decision, replayable

What this is. A recording of one dotc run, replayed in linked views with one shared cursor (the event axis and the phase bands under it).

Explorer (left): units, their classes and members, with how much the current phase changed each of them (and in total); tick several to show them together.

Phase scrubber (middle): the printed tree after each phase (source text for the parser), nodes that changed are underlined by kind of change; hover shows where a node came from. With JVM bytecode in the recording the phase list continues into the optimiser's passes.

Event inspector (top right): the typer's decisions (typing, adaptation, implicit search with candidates, completions) for the selected node, with filters and a Reports chip.

Dependencies (bottom right): what the selected unit depends on, as a table of neighbours by kind with weights and a sparkline of when each edge was established, plus a summary of what it pulled in; the graph of the neighbourhood is collapsed under it.

Phases table (▦ in the header) ranks phases by how much they changed; i / I jump between the most interesting ones. View options (⚙) holds the toggles; ? lists the keys.
